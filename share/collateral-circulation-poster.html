The brain's backup routes

Linked arteries and smaller connecting vessels can help maintain blood flow when a route narrows or is blocked. Their anatomy and effectiveness vary from person to person.

What is the Circle of Willis?

A ring-like set of arteries at the brain's base links major anterior and posterior circulation routes. Its completeness and vessel size vary widely.

What are leptomeningeal collaterals?

Small connections on the brain surface can provide alternate routes between arterial territories when flow is reduced.

What can collateral flow do?

Collateral routes may help sustain perfusion and are associated with the amount of threatened tissue in ischemic stroke. They are not guaranteed protection and do not replace urgent treatment.

Can I strengthen this network?

There is no proven habit that guarantees a complete collateral network. Following evidence-based cardiovascular health guidance, including blood-pressure management and not smoking, supports overall vascular health.

Stroke is an emergency. Sudden face drooping, arm weakness, speech difficulty, vision loss, imbalance, or severe unexplained headache? Call your local emergency number immediately. Do not wait to see whether symptoms pass.
