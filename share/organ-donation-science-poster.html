How does matching happen?

In the United States, the Organ Procurement and Transplantation Network (OPTN) supports the national waiting list and matching process. Transplant teams assess medical suitability and match factors.

Who can register?

People can learn about registration and donor eligibility through the official U.S. donation registry information. Age or medical history alone does not necessarily rule someone out; suitability is assessed at the time of donation.

What can I do today?

Learn how donation works, check the rules where you live, make your choice through an official registry if you decide to register, and talk with loved ones about your decision.

Every donation is individual. The “up to” figures describe potential impact, not a promise; the number of people helped depends on medical suitability and the type of donation.
